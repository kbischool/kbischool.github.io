kbi://about/welcome

Welcome to KBI Schools

Knowledge Base International Schools (KBI Schools) is a private institution in Moniya, Ibadan, providing quality education from Crèche through Senior Secondary School (KBI College) since 2012. We pair a solid academic foundation with discipline, character formation, and hands‑on exposure to technology — preparing every child not just to pass an exam, but to think, create, and lead.

At KBI Schools, we believe true education goes beyond the classroom. We are committed to a safe, supportive and stimulating environment where every child discovers their potential, builds confidence, thinks critically, and prepares for a successful future — guided by our motto, Knowledge in Humility.

MISSION

Our Mission

To provide a safe, disciplined and stimulating learning environment where every child — from Crèche through Senior Secondary — receives a values-driven education that develops them academically, morally, socially and technologically, and prepares them to excel in examinations and in life.

VISION

Our Vision

To be a leading indigenous school in Oyo State, recognised for academic excellence, strong moral character, and the production of disciplined, critical-thinking, technologically-empowered young leaders who compete confidently on any stage.

kbi://why-us

Why families choose KBI Schools

01 · academics

Academic excellence

A structured curriculum from Crèche to SSS3 built around Common Entrance, BECE, WAEC/NECO and JAMB success, supported by scheme-of-work-driven teaching and regular mock examinations.

02 · character

Moral & discipline

Character formation is treated as seriously as academics — honesty, respect and personal responsibility, with clear, firm but fair standards across every section.

03 · history

Proven track record

Since 2012, KBI Schools has grown from a small nursery/primary outfit into a full Crèche-to-SSS3 institution, guiding students through Common Entrance, BECE and WAEC/NECO with transparent fee communication.

04 · campus

Facilities

Purpose-built classrooms, an ICT/Digital Technologies lab, a library, a science laboratory, an outdoor sports field and a multipurpose hall for assemblies and ceremonies.

05 · staffing

Qualified staffing

A blended team of experienced permanent teachers and NYSC corps members — many of whom stay connected to the school long after service through our Ex‑Corpers network.

06 · practicals

Hands-on practicals

STEM, Coding & Robotics education plus a Prevocational Skills programme covering arts & crafts, household products and pastry-making — real exposure to the tools of a changing world.

flagship programme

STEM, Coding & Robotics

Introduced school-wide from the 2023/2024 session. Pupils code in tools like Scratch and Micro:bit MakeCode, and work through age-graded group projects — including a JSS1/JSS2 unit on the history of robotics. Computer Studies was upgraded and renamed Digital Technologies, now compulsory for every student.
