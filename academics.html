Academics

Nigeria's national curriculum, enriched with STEM, Coding & Robotics and Digital Technologies at every level.

Curriculum

KBI Schools follows the Nigerian national curriculum across all levels — Crèche, Nursery, Primary and Secondary — enriched with STEM, Coding & Robotics and Digital Technologies (formerly Computer Studies, now compulsory school-wide). Lessons are guided by structured schemes of work reviewed each term.

Two flagship enrichment programmes run alongside the academic curriculum:

  • STEM, Coding & Robotics — programming across multiple languages, plus hands-on Scratch and Micro:bit MakeCode work
  • Prevocational Skills — arts & crafts, production of household products, pastry-making, and other entrepreneurial trades
recent curriculum updates
  • Basic Technology + Basic Science merged into Intermediate Science (JSS)
  • Social Studies + Civic Education merged into Social & Citizenship Studies (JSS)
  • Computer Studies renamed Digital Technologies, now compulsory
  • Civic Education renamed Citizenship & Heritage Studies (SSS)
  • Animal Husbandry remains the school's primary Trade subject
