# Student guide

The KBI Portal lets you see your own attendance, progress and published report cards. You can only look. You cannot change anything.

## First time
1. Open https://kbischool.github.io/portal/ and choose **Create an account**. Use your own email address, or Google, and confirm your email.
2. Choose **I am a student**. Enter your full name.
3. Press **Link my record**. Type your admission number and the access code from the school office.
4. Your **Progress**, **Report cards** and **Attendance** tabs appear. Report cards show only after the school releases the term.

## If the code does not work
Ask the school office for a new code. A code stops working when the school replaces or revokes it.
