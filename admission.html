Admission

Everything you need to apply — class structure, entrance assessments, documents and fees.

Prospectus

KBI Schools admits pupils from Crèche through Senior Secondary School (SSS3). We welcome applications from families who value premium education, sound moral character and holistic development.

feesFee transparency. KBI Schools itemises every charge so parents know exactly what they are paying for — no hidden costs.
siblingsSibling discount. Families with two or more children enrolled enjoy a fee discount.
meritScholarships. A merit-based scholarship programme rewards high-performing students, reviewed each session based on assessment performance.

Exact fee schedules, discount percentages and scholarship thresholds are confirmed each session — please contact the school office for the current published figures.

Application Procedure

  1. Obtain the admission form

    Complete it online via "Apply Here" or in person at the school office.

  2. Sit the entrance / placement exam

    Scheduled during term holidays for new entrants into Nursery through Secondary; JSS1 entrants also sit the Common Entrance / Placement-Screening exam where applicable.

  3. Receive placement communication

    Successful candidates are notified and placed into the appropriate class.

  4. Scholarship track (optional)

    Qualifying JSS1 entrants may sit a dedicated scholarship examination alongside the standard entrance exams, with awards based on merit.

  5. Complete registration

    Submit required documents and pay fees to secure your ward's place.

documents typically required
  • Completed admission form
  • Two recent passport photographs
  • Birth certificate or age declaration
  • Previous school's testimonial / report card (transfers)
  • Immunisation record (Crèche / Nursery applicants)

Common questions

Quick answers before you apply. Tap a question to expand it.

Is there a fee discount for siblings?

Yes. Families with two or more children enrolled at KBI Schools enjoy a sibling discount on fees — see the Prospectus above for the fee-transparency policy.

Do all new pupils sit an entrance exam?

New entrants into Nursery through Secondary sit an entrance/placement exam during term holidays. JSS1 entrants also sit the Common Entrance / Placement-Screening exam where applicable.

What documents do I need to apply?

A completed admission form, two recent passport photographs, the child's birth certificate or age declaration, a previous school's testimonial/report card for transfers, and an immunisation record for Crèche/Nursery applicants.

Are scholarships available?

Yes — a merit-based scholarship programme rewards high-performing students, reviewed each session based on assessment performance.

What age groups does KBI Schools admit?

From Crèche (6 months–1 year) right through to SSS 3 (14–16 years) — see the full class structure & age guide above. Age bands are indicative; final placement follows the entrance assessment.

Ready to register your child?

Admission is open across Crèche, Nursery, Primary and Secondary (KBI College). Our team will walk you through the entrance assessment and placement process.
