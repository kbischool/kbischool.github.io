Fee and school-information portal

Choose how you use the portal

The same sign-in page serves everyone. What you see depends on the account the school has set up for you.

Parents

  • Your child’s fees, itemised, with how much has been paid
  • Past sessions’ fee history and your own payment history
  • Textbooks to collect and the notebooks and stationery list

School staff

  • Find any student and see who has completed payment and who is owing
  • Past sessions’ fee records
  • New-student invoices and stock quantities

School management

  • School-wide totals and trends
  • Payment records and data imports
  • Accounts and access for parents and staff
Open the fee portal (opens in a new tab) Register for portal access

The portal is private. You can only see the records your own account is allowed to see.

How to sign in

Three steps

The portal works on a phone, a tablet or a computer, and prints cleanly. It has light and dark themes.

  1. Open the portal

    Use the button above, or go to the address in your browser’s bar. Bookmark it for next time.

  2. Sign in

    Use the email or Google account the school registered for you. Parents may be asked for the parent code the school issued.

  3. Cannot get in?

    Contact the school office. Accounts and codes are set up by the school, and codes do not change.
