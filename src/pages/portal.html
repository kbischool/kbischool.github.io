One portal, five roles

What each person can do

Everyone signs in at the same place. What you see depends on your role, and each person sees only what their role allows.

School owner

  • See the whole school and every admin, teacher and student account
  • Verify the school and appoint or replace the admin
  • Read the audit log and manage settings and holidays

Admins

  • Set up classes, subjects, the term and score weights
  • Manage students, approve teachers and issue access codes
  • Release report cards to parents and students when a term is ready

Teachers

  • Take the daily morning and afternoon attendance register
  • Enter continuous assessment and exam scores for their subjects
  • Class teachers prepare report cards with conduct ratings and comments

Students

  • See their own attendance and progress
  • Read published report cards
  • View only: students cannot change any record

Parents

  • Follow each child’s attendance and results, even with children in different classes
  • Read and print published report cards
  • Send an absence note to the class teacher and keep family and health details up to date
Open the KBI Portal Create an account

The portal is private. Records are protected by role, and you can only see what your own account is allowed to see.

How to sign in

Four steps

The portal works on a phone, a tablet or a computer, installs like an app, and keeps working offline for the pages you have opened. It has light and dark themes.

  1. Create an account

    Use the button above, then confirm your email address. You can also sign in with Google.

  2. Choose your role

    Pick parent, student or teacher. Teachers wait for the school admin to approve them.

  3. Link your record

    Parents and students enter the admission number and the access code from the school office.

  4. Cannot get in?

    Contact the school office. Admins and the school owner are set up by the school.

Linked service

Fees & Accounts

School fees, payments and stock are kept in a separate system for now. Parents see itemised fees and payment history, and staff see who has paid. It will be brought into the KBI Portal later.
