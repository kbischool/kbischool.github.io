What happens next

After you submit

  1. The school office receives it

    Your details go straight to the school’s email inbox. Nothing is stored on this website.

  2. We contact you

    Admissions will call or email to arrange the entrance assessment. For portal requests, we send you the admission number and access code.

  3. Need help sooner?

    Call +234 812 690 9498 or message us on WhatsApp (opens in a new tab).
