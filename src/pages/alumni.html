Our alumni

KBI Schools is proud of the network of people who have passed through our doors, including a strong community of former NYSC corps members who taught at the school and remain connected long after their service year through our active Ex‑Corpers network.

We are equally proud of our growing body of student alumni, many of whom have gone on to tertiary institutions across Nigeria and beyond. As our alumni network grows, this page will feature their stories: where they have gone, what they have achieved, and how their time at KBI shaped their path.
