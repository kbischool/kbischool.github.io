Add KBI Schools to your iPhone

iPhones have no install button, but it only takes three taps in Safari.

  1. Tap the Share button

    It is the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen (or the top on iPad).

  2. Choose “Add to Home Screen”

    Scroll down the list of options until you see it.

  3. Tap “Add”

    The KBI Schools crest now appears on your home screen like any other app.
