Learning beyond the classroom

Our facilities are designed to support academic learning, practical skills, sports, health, safety and technology-driven education.

Academic Support

Classrooms and a library that support structured learning and independent study.

STEM & Digital Learning

An ICT/Digital Technologies lab and Smart Classroom model supporting Coding & Robotics.

Sports & Physical Development

An environment designed for growth

  • Purpose-built classrooms supporting focused learning at every level, from Crèche through SSS3
  • A science laboratory supporting Physics, Chemistry and Biology practicals
  • An ICT/Digital Technologies lab supporting Coding, Robotics and computer-based learning
  • A library supporting research and independent reading
  • An auditorium/multipurpose hall for assemblies, Cultural Day and graduation ceremonies
  • A secure, gated compound with a dedicated security officer on site
